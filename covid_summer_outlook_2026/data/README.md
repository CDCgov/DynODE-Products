### Folder Content
- **`demographic-newcensus-data/`**: total population of all states, age stratified population for example population, and contact matrices.
- **`hospitalization-data/`**: weekly hospitalization data for example population.
- **`initialization-data/`**: estimated bins for the SEIS compartment model used in DynODE
- **`vaccination-data/`**: equations that are fit to state vaccination data.
- **`variant-data/`**: weekly variant proportions of infections by state.
